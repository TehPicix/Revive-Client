Features

Everything you need past spawn

2b2t has no rules and no mercy. Revive gives you the movement, awareness, and automation to make the trip out — and stay alive when you get there.

🪽

Highway Runner

Elytra automation and pathfinding tuned for the axis highways. Cover a million blocks without touching the keyboard.

📡

Player Radar

ESP, tracers, and proximity alerts so you know someone's near before they load your base. Silence isn't safety.

💎

Crystal PvP

Auto-crystal, auto-totem, surround, and hole detection built for the end-crystal meta that decides every fight.

⏳

Queue Manager

Auto-reconnect, live queue position, and AFK-safe idling so a long wait never costs you your slot.

🧭

Stash Finder

Chunk-based stash and base scanning while you travel — turn every trip into a raiding run.

🎛️

Clean HUD

Draggable click-GUI, module search, and config profiles you can actually read at 20fps in a trashed chunk.

The launcher

Log in. Press play. Done.

No juggling JARs or Fabric installers. The Revive launcher handles the modloader, keeps the client updated, and lets you drop in your own mods like Litematica.

  • +
    One-click playSign in with your Revive account, hit play, and you're in Minecraft — the launcher wires up Fabric for you.
  • +
    Auto-updating clientClient mods are installed and kept current automatically. No re-downloading on every patch.
  • +
    Bring your own modsAdd any Fabric mod for the current version — Litematica, MaLiLib, whatever your setup needs.
  • +
    Make it yoursTheme the launcher with custom accent colors and tune your RAM in settings.

Why 2b2t

Not another minigame client

Most clients are tuned for Hypixel. Revive is built for the one server where the terrain itself is the enemy — queue-aware, lag-tolerant, highway-first.

🔁

Queue-aware

Handles the 2b2t priority and main queue natively, with reconnect logic that respects the server instead of fighting it.

⚡

Lag-tolerant

Built to stay responsive in the entity-flooded, trashed chunks near spawn where other clients choke.

💾

Config that survives

Profile system keeps your base coords and combat settings intact through every update.

⚠ Know the risk

Third-party clients violate Minecraft's EULA and can get your account banned on most servers. 2b2t is anarchy and allows them, but you run this at your own risk. Revive is a hobby project in active development — expect bugs.
